This is where I keep my work, my writing, and whatever I'm thinking about. None of it is finished. Neither am I. Look around.

I'm Yeboah. I grew up between Torugbene, Warri, and Yenagoa in southern Nigeria. I studied political science, got into governance research, and ended up covering African tech startups for a living. Along the way, I co-founded a governance non-profit, started a civic tech project, and moved to Morocco for a master's degree.

Right now, I'm in Rabat, finishing a master's in collective intelligence at UM6P and working as a research intern at Africa Business School.
